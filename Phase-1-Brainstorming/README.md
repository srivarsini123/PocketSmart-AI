# Phase 1 - Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Name
PocketSmart AI - Your Smart Budget and Recommendation Assistant

## 1. Problem Identification

Many users find it difficult to manage their monthly income,
track their expenses and plan their budget effectively.

## 2. Proposed Solution

PocketSmart AI is a smart budgeting and recommendation assistant
that helps users track expenses, calculate their remaining balance
and receive AI-based budget suggestions.

## 3. Objectives

- To help users track their income and expenses.
- To calculate total spending and remaining balance.
- To provide simple budget suggestions.
- To analyze spending patterns.
- To provide personalized recommendations based on user inputs.

## 4. Target Users

- Students
- Working professionals
- Individuals who want to manage their personal budget

## 5. Main Features

- Monthly income entry
- Expense tracking
- Category-wise expense analysis
- Remaining balance calculation
- AI-powered budget suggestions
- Smart recommendations
- Simple web interface

## 6. Expected Outcome

PocketSmart AI will provide users with a simple and interactive
assistant for understanding and managing their personal budget.
